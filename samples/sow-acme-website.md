# Statement of Work: Acme Co website redesign

**Vendor:** Sam Rivera (freelance designer and developer)
**Client:** Acme Co, contact Dana Lee (Marketing Lead)
**Signed:** 2026-10-20 · **Start:** 2026-10-27 · **Delivery:** 2026-12-05

## §1 Purpose
Redesign and rebuild Acme Co's marketing website on the client's existing WordPress hosting.

## §2 Deliverables
- §2.1 **Homepage**: hero section, three feature blocks, testimonials and footer.
- §2.2 **About**: company story and a team grid of up to 8 people.
- §2.3 **Services**: one page listing up to 6 services.
- §2.4 **Blog index**: a listing template. Individual posts use the theme's default post template.
- §2.5 **Contact**: a contact form that sends to up to 2 email addresses, plus an embedded map.

## §3 Page count
The site has the **5 pages listed in §2**. Additional pages or templates are out of scope.

## §4 Revisions
Each deliverable in §2 includes **2 revision rounds**. A round is one consolidated list of feedback sent in a single message. Feedback after the second round is billed as a change order.

## §5 Exclusions
The following are not included: copywriting, stock photo purchases, logo or brand identity work, SEO migration or redirects, hosting, e-commerce, and multilingual versions.

## §6 Rate card
| Work type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Design | $60 / hour |
| Development | $75 / hour |
| Minimum change order | 1 hour |

## §7 Change process
Out-of-scope work requires a written change order. The client accepts the change order in the project chat, and the vendor approves it before work starts. Work is not started on unapproved change orders.

## §8 AI assistant
The vendor uses an AI assistant (Scope Guard) that reads the messages and files in the project chat and compares requests with this agreement. It never posts in the project chat, never contacts the client, and never makes decisions: every reply, price and change order comes from the vendor. Messages are processed by the vendor's AI provider. The client may ask the vendor at any time to turn it off for this project.
